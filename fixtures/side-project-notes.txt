Quick paste about a hackathon project in case it's useful:

Built "Shelfie" at the 2024 Austin Hack Day with two other people — a phone
app that scans a bookshelf and tells you what to read next. I did the image
pipeline (on-device OCR + a small embedding model to match spines to a book
database). We won second place out of ~40 teams. It's not live anywhere,
just a demo.

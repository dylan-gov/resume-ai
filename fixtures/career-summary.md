## Side project: Routeplan

Built and still maintain **Routeplan**, a route-optimization tool for small
delivery fleets (routeplan.example.com). Started it in 2023 as a weekend
project, now has ~200 active users.

- Wrote the solver from scratch (a variant of nearest-neighbor + 2-opt)
- Handles ~5k stops per run in under 2 seconds
- Stack: TypeScript, Postgres, deployed on Fly.io
